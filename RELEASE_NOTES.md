AI Architectures for desktop: the CAD studio and quick renders in their own window. The app always runs the latest version of the sites, so it never needs updating for new features.

**1.0.3:** The window always has its close, minimise and maximise buttons (traffic lights on Mac) and opens fully on screen, also on smaller screens or after a monitor change. **File → Quit** (Ctrl+Q, Cmd+Q on Mac), Ctrl+W and Alt+F4 close it, also while you draw. In the CAD studio, the 3D / render panel can now be dragged wider or narrower (double-click the divider to reset, drag it shut to hide it), and only one pointer shows on the drawing.

**1.0.2:** The Mac app is signed by 20Vision GmbH and notarized by Apple, so it opens with a double-click (no "unidentified developer" warning).

**1.0.1:** “Sign in with Google” now works: it opens your browser, you sign in there, and the app is signed in.

**Download**
- **Windows:** `AI-Architectures-Setup-x64.exe` (ARM PCs: `-arm64.exe`). Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **macOS** (Apple silicon and Intel): `AI-Architectures-mac-universal.dmg`, drag the app into Applications. The first time, right-click the app → **Open** → **Open** (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**).
- **Linux:** `.AppImage` (make it executable, double-click) or `.deb` (`sudo apt install ./AI-Architectures-…deb`).
